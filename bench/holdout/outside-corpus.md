# Gold cases from outside the corpus (2026-11-07)

Five tree figures from other animal groups, to test whether the tool generalises beyond dinosaur figures. They were chosen, not drawn at random: open-access PLOS ONE papers (all CC BY 4.0) on frogs, decapod crustaceans, swallowtail and nymphalid butterflies and bats, found through Europe PMC's search, keeping figures whose tip names are legible at the published size. Three others were passed over because their tip names were too small to read (a scarab-beetle mitogenome tree, a cyprinodontiform fish tree and a 100-plus-tip bombardier-beetle tree), and one because some of its collapsed clades are unlabelled (an orthalicoid snail summary tree).

| shi2015_pone_fig4 | nymphalid butterflies | 36 | panel A of two trees (region) |

Each case folder holds the journal's figure image (`figure.png`, unmodified), `attribution.txt`, `case.json` (licence and attribution), `names.txt` (the printed tip names) and `gold.nex` (topology only, transcribed from the figure and checked node by node). Tips are the printed text with `_` for spaces. Brackets are dropped and repeated names numbered, because Newick names can hold neither; each case's gold_note lists the changes.
